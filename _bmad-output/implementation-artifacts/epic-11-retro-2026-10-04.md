## Open questions

All are unresolved and await Otavio. They come from the spec's "Decisions (made unattended, for Otavio to check)" and this retro.

1. **"Not checked" warns and lets the operator continue; it does not refuse** (decision 1). AD-13 is met either way. Refusing would block import without a connection. Should an unreadable account refuse instead?
2. **New copy** (decision 2): "Master key disabled" / "Master key not checked", "The seed has not been saved yet.", "Cancel import" / "Import anyway" in place of "Got it". The Settings dialog title is still "Heads up", while Onboarding dropped it (`c2-03` vs `c1-03`). Is that inconsistency intended?
3. **Interior whitespace is rejected, not stripped** (decision 3). This includes zero-width characters (F5).
4. **The 15 s bound on the pre-flight check** (decision 4).
5. **The Settings Add dialog cannot be closed while a check or write runs** (decision 5).
6. **Onboarding still sets the PIN and passkey before probing** (decision 6). This is what creates F3. Should the probe move ahead of vault setup?
7. **"Import anyway" carries the primary (filled) emphasis on both screens**, and "Cancel import" is outlined (`c1-03`, `c2-03`, `c3-01`). Should the action that proceeds past a warning be the visually default one? This is a design question, not a defect.
