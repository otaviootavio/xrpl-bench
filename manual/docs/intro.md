# XRPL Bench user manual

XRPL Bench is a self-custody XRP Ledger wallet that runs in your browser as a
PWA. There is no backend: your keys stay on your device and the ledger is the
only server.

This manual is for people who already know the XRPL's vocabulary — drops,
reserves, trust lines, sequence numbers. It explains what the wallet does and
how to do each task, not what the ledger is.

:::warning[Real funds]
This wallet is meant to hold real Mainnet funds. Practise every flow on Testnet
first.
:::
