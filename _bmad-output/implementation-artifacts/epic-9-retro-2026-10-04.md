### Process lessons

- **P1: The unattended loop had no path for "a decision the reviewer says Otavio must take".** The reviewer did exactly what the loop asks: it marked the point non-blocking and asked for it among the decisions. The resolver recorded it in the spec and then merged. Nothing held the merge on the choice, and the PR body was not regenerated. F1 is a money-moving warning that stays silent, and it is now on `dev` by default. A "for Otavio" decision that weakens a money-moving guard needs either a hold on merge or a hold on promotion.
- **P2: The PR body and the spec drifted.** The body is the summary Otavio reads; the spec is the record. They disagree on the decision count (8 vs 9). Review fixes that touch decisions must update both.
- **P3: The browser-pass definition of done held.** Epic 5 item 7 was followed: the spec records the build, the driver, the widths, the themes and per-criterion results. The pass found a real copy defect (self-review triage #9) that the gates had passed. This is the epic 5 lesson working.
- **P4: An AC can encode a hazard.** 9.1's "existing entries read as tagless" was written at planning time (2026-09-15). Nobody then traced what a tagless reading means for FR-21 on the tagless send. The fresh reviewer found it at merge time.
