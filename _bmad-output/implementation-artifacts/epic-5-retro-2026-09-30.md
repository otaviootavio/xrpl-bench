## Acceptance verdict

**Machine verdict: rejected.** The criteria are declared (the story ACs in `epics.md` §Epic 5 and the requirements in `epic-5-context.md`).

- All five stories are `done`, and each story's own acceptance criteria are met in the evidence. Criteria 5.3 AC 2 and 5.4-mobile failed in the browser and are fixed and re-verified (`3acd048`, `bfc4c29`). 5.5 passed on Testnet.
- **Blocking: F1.** The epic's first requirement, "A guard on a money-moving action fails closed … an errored read … fail[s] it", does not hold for token sends. A failed trust-line read lets an issued payment through (`SendTab.tsx:256`). The epic's goal statement names exactly this class of defect.
- **Not demonstrated: 5.1's Testnet criterion.** 5.1 was never exercised in a browser (`spec-5-1-destination-check-guard.md` Verification), and neither was 5.2.
- Open, non-blocking: F2–F5, and the deferred ledger (P5).

**Human decision (Otavio, 2026-10-02): accepted-with-open-items.** This overrides the machine verdict. F1 and the missing 5.1/5.2 browser passes are carried as tracked open items (action items 1 and 6), not as blockers. The final recorded verdict is **accepted-with-open-items**.
